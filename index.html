About Zen Roofing

Calm, careful roofing for a city of fog, wind and rain.

San Francisco roofs take a beating: salty marine air, winter storms and decades-old Victorian framing with valleys, dormers and chimneys at every turn. Those joints are where leaks start, so that's where we slow down.

We're a veteran-owned roofing contractor based on Scott Street in the Western Addition. We bring the same discipline to every job: clear communication, an honest price and detailed work that keeps the water out.

What we do

Roofing services, start to finish.

From a single dripping skylight to a full tear-off and replacement, every job gets the same attention to detail.

01

Roof Repair & Leak Detection

We track down the real source of the leak, repair it properly and seal the surrounding area so it doesn't come back.

02

Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and new installation for homes and multi-unit buildings, with materials matched to your home and budget.

03

Custom Metal Flashing

Flashing and sealing fabricated to fit your roof's valleys, walls and transitions, where most leaks begin.

04

Skylight Sealing

Skylights are prone to leaks. We re-flash and seal them so you keep the light and lose the drips.

05

Chimney Flashing

Brick chimneys shift and crack their seals over time. We install step and counter flashing that stays watertight.

06

24/7 Emergency Service

Storm damage or a sudden leak at 2am? Call us any time and we'll help protect your home.
